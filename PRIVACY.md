# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Circadian Guard (the "app") is an open-source Android app designed with privacy as a priority. The defining property of the app is that **all data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server.**

## Information we handle

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Illuminance (lux) | Recording and visualizing ambient light | On-device only |
| Timestamp | Ordering recordings over time | On-device only |
| Location (approximate, while in use) | Associating a "place" | On-device only |
| Screen brightness setting (optional) | Supplementary recording | On-device only |

## Storage and transmission

- **All data is stored on the device only and is never transmitted to or uploaded from any external server.**
- Precise (fine) location is not collected.
- Only approximate (coarse) location is used, and coordinates are not retained persistently after place association.

## Legal basis for processing

We process data solely on the basis of your **consent**, which you give by installing and using the app and by granting the relevant permissions (approximate location, notifications). Because all processing happens on your own device and no data is ever sent to us, we simply provide software that runs locally for your benefit.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because your data never leaves your device, you retain full, direct control over it:

- **Access** — you can view your data at any time in the app.
- **Rectification and erasure** — you can delete all data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
- **Portability** — on-device export is planned for a future release.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, these arrangements give practical effect to your rights of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability under the GDPR. We aim to comply with applicable data protection and privacy laws, including the GDPR.

## Data retention

Data is retained on your device until you delete it. There is no automatic deletion and no server-side copy.

## Third parties, advertising, tracking

- The app contains **no ads, analytics, trackers, or third-party SDKs**.
- No data is shared with any third party.

## Not a medical device

This app is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or assess health conditions.
